节律感知 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 12 日 · 版本 1.0.0

1. 概述

本隐私政策适用于「节律感知」iOS 应用（以下简称「本应用」）。本应用是 urt-01 毫米波节律感知设备的配套客户端，用于连接设备并查看设备测量的心率与呼吸数值。我们重视你的隐私，本应用不收集、不上传任何个人数据。

2. 我们处理的信息

  • 蓝牙数据：心率、呼吸数值通过低功耗蓝牙（BLE）在设备与手机之间传输，仅在手机内存中实时显示，不写入持久化存储，不上传至任何服务器。
  • 设备信息：设备名称、信号强度、固件版本仅在本地用于连接与维护展示，不会离开你的设备。
  • 固件下载：当你主动发起固件升级时，本应用会访问固件服务器下载固件文件；该请求不包含任何个人数据。

3. 系统权限

蓝牙：用于搜索、连接 urt-01 设备并接收实时数据。你可以随时在系统设置中关闭该权限，关闭后将无法使用设备连接功能。

4. 数据共享与第三方

本应用不与任何第三方共享数据，不集成广告、统计分析或追踪 SDK，不进行跨应用追踪。

5. 数据保留

本应用不保留任何个人数据。实时读数仅存在于内存中，退出应用后即被清除。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何用户数据，包括儿童的个人数据。

7. 医疗声明

本产品为消费级健康产品，测量结果仅供参考，不构成医疗建议，不用于疾病诊断或治疗。如有健康问题，请咨询专业医疗人员。

8. 政策更新

如本政策发生变更，我们会在本页面更新版本号与生效日期。

9. 联系我们

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the "节律感知" iOS app (the "App"), the companion client for the urt-01 millimeter-wave rhythm sensing device. The App does not collect or upload any personal data.

2. Information We Process

  • Bluetooth data: Heart rate and breathing rate are transferred between the device and your phone over Bluetooth Low Energy, displayed in memory only, never persisted, and never uploaded to any server.
  • Device information: Device name, signal strength, and firmware version are shown locally for connection and maintenance purposes only.
  • Firmware download: When you start a firmware update, the App downloads the firmware file from our server. The request contains no personal data.

3. Permissions

Bluetooth: Used to discover, connect to, and receive real-time data from the urt-01 device. You may disable this permission in system settings at any time; device connectivity will stop working.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

The App does not share data with any third party and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

5. Data Retention

The App retains no personal data. Live readings exist only in memory and are cleared when the App exits.

6. Children's Privacy

The App collects no user data, including data from children.

7. Medical Disclaimer

This product is a consumer wellness product. Measurements are for reference only and are not medical advice; they must not be used to diagnose or treat any condition.

8. Changes

We will update the version and effective date on this page if this policy changes.
